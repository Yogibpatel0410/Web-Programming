Golf Tips

Golfers use different clubs depending on the type of shot they need to make. Each club is designed for a different distance and situation.

Drivers are normally used for long shots from the tee, while irons are used for medium-distance shots toward the green.

Putters are used on the green to roll the golf ball into the hole with accuracy and control.
