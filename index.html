Golf Tips

Golf is a popular sport played on courses around the world. The goal is to hit a golf ball into each hole using the fewest number of strokes possible.

A normal golf course usually contains 18 holes. Each hole can include fairways, bunkers, rough areas, trees, and putting greens that challenge the golfer.

Golf is enjoyed by beginners and professionals because it combines skill, patience, strategy, and accuracy.
